# Phylaworld

> **A pixel art, top-down 2D multiplayer world where creatures are tamed, studied, trained — and fought with in turn-based battles.**

Welcome to Phylaworld, the *mainline* game of an evolving creature universe. It's a living world where you and other players tame wild creatures, research and understand them, train them to be the best they can be, and pit them against each other in strategic turn-based battles and contests. Elemental type match-ups, unique abilities, individual natures, breeding, trading, personalization, farming, and base creation all come together — with regional and global challenges to conquer in teams or solo.

This is not just a game. Phylaworld is the foundation for an entire connected franchise. The creatures you meet here will follow you across many more games and applications, carrying their history with them wherever they go.

## About

Phylaworld is a **pixel art, top-down 2D multiplayer** adventure. At its heart is a simple, timeless fantasy: the bond between a person and the wild creatures of their world.

You start in a vibrant region — teaming up with friends or venturing out solo — taming creatures you find in the wild, learning to understand them, training them, and competing in turn-based battles and contests. Every creature is unique, with its own elemental affinities, abilities, and personality. As you explore, you'll research new species, breed generations with desirable traits, trade rare finds with other players, farm resources, build a base to call home, and take on challenges that span your region and the whole world.

Phylaworld is designed to be **community-first** and **AI-friendly**, built entirely on open and free tooling, and is the foundation stone of a much larger connected universe of games and applications.

## Key Features

### Taming & The Wild

The world of Phylaworld is full of wild creatures. Not every creature will let you walk up and befriend it — taming takes patience, observation, and care. Learn each species' behavior, win their trust, and bring them into your team. Success isn't guaranteed; every wild encounter is a fresh, individual moment.

### Research & Study

Creatures are mysterious, and knowledge is power. Research each species to unlock its lore, understand its strengths and weaknesses, and uncover hidden potential. The more you study with a creature, the more its capabilities and history unfold.

### Training & Development

Taming is only the beginning. Train your creatures to grow stronger, faster, and more skilled. A well-trained creature with a strong bond will outperform one that's simply been caught and forgotten.

### Turn-Based Battles & Contests

When it's time to compete, battles play out as **strategic, turn-based contests**. Positioning, timing, type advantages, ability choices, and squad composition all matter. It's a game of reading your opponent and playing to your creatures' strengths — not just raw numbers.

### Elemental Type Match-ups

Every creature belongs to elemental types, and types interact. Fire scorches grass; water douses flame. Master the match-up system to gain a critical edge in battle, and think carefully about the types you send into the fight.

### Unique Abilities

Beyond type, each creature has unique abilities that bend the rules of battle. These signature moves define a creature's identity and give you a deeper layer of strategy beyond the type chart.

### Individual Natures

No two creatures are identical. Each one has an **individual nature** that shapes its personality and stat tendencies — making one creature subtly different from another of the same species. Natures add depth to training, breeding, and team building.

### Breeding

Breed your creatures to combine traits, improve stats, and pass on desirable abilities. Breeding is where long-term strategy and patience pay off — and where truly exceptional creatures are born.

### Trading

The world is more than one player. Trade creatures with friends and strangers, hunt for rare species, and build bonds across the community. Trading is a pillar of the Phylaworld experience.

### Personalization

Make your creatures and your presence felt. Customize appearances, give creatures personality, and express yourself across the world.

### Farming

Grow resources to support your journey. Farming provides the materials and income you need to feed, train, and expand — a calm, rewarding counterpoint to taming and battle.

### Base Creation

Stake your claim. Build and customize a base that reflects who you are — a home for you and your creatures, a workshop, a showcase for your collection.

### Challenges: Regional & Global

The world is alive with goals. Take on **regional challenges** in your own area, and rise to **global challenges** that involve players around the world. Compete individually or contribute as part of a larger team effort.

### Multiplayer: Team & Solo

Phylaworld is multiplayer at its core. Play **solo** at your own pace, or team up with friends and the wider community to tackle goals no single player could achieve alone. Your choice, your rhythm.

## The Connected Universe

Phylaworld is the **mainline** game of a wider, ongoing universe. The world, its lore, and especially its **creatures** are shared resources that will carry across an expanding family of games and applications.

### Planned Derivative Projects

While Phylaworld remains the primary focus, plans exist for future projects built on this shared universe, including:

- **A Trading Card Game** based on Phylaworld creatures and tactics.
- **An Online Tournament Game** focused on competitive battles.
- **A Multiplayer Online Battle Arena (MOBA)** featuring creature-based team combat.
- **An AR/VR Virtual Pet App Companion** — take your creatures with you into the real world.
- **An Art-Centric Game** focused on the world's visual identity and creativity.
- **An MMORPG** expanding the world into a persistent, massively shared space.
- **A Management / Idle Game** built around running your farm and base.
- **A First-Person Battler** giving a new perspective on creature battles.
- **Education-Focused Games** using the world and its creatures as a learning vehicle.

These projects are intentional roads on the horizon, not immediate commitments — each will be scoped and developed on its own schedule. Together they form a franchise where every game enriches the others.

### Cross-Game Creature Progression

A defining feature of the connected universe is **persistent, synced progression across all games**.

- **Carry your creatures anywhere.** If you tame a creature in the mainline series, you can transfer it freely to other games and applications in the franchise.
- **History travels with you.** A creature's progress in one game earns it something in another — a **medal, bonus stats, a rare move or ability, or another characteristic** that depends on which game it earned the distinction in.
- **The return trip matters too.** Transferring a creature *into* a game can unlock features and resources linked to that creature, deepening the sense of a shared, living collection.

Progress on all games and apps will be **samely synced**, so your collection evolves as a single, continuous story no matter where you choose to play.

## Technology Stack

Phylaworld is built exclusively on free and open software, so that anyone — human or AI assistant — can learn it, use it, and contribute to it without cost.

### Game Engine & Editor

**[Godot Engine v4.x](https://godotengine.org)** — a free, open-source, MIT-licensed game engine. Godot serves as both the game engine and the editor for every project in the universe. The project targets the latest stable Godot 4 release line.

### 2D Game Assets

**[Aseprite](https://www.aseprite.org)** — the go-to tool for crafting the game's pixel art sprites, tiles, and animations.

### Key & Concept Art

**[Krita](https://krita.org/en/)**, **[GIMP](https://www.gimp.org)**, and **[Blender](https://www.blender.org)** — used for key art, concept art, and exploratory visual design.

### Music & Sound Effects

The final pipeline for music and SFX is **yet to be determined**, but strong candidates are:

- **[FamiStudio](https://famistudio.org/)** — retro-style synth & chiptune composition.
- **[Dn-FamiTracker](https://github.com/Dn-Programming-Core-Management/Dn-FamiTracker/)** — chiptune tracker.
- **[Ardour](https://ardour.org/)** — full-featured digital audio workstation.
- **[LMMS](https://lmms.io)** — cross-platform music production suite.

All are free/open options, keeping the project aligned with its open-source ethos.

## AI-Friendly Development

**This project is explicitly AI-friendly.** We welcome AI assistants, code-generation tools, AI-driven art and audio pipelines, and automated development workflows at every level. The project deliberately uses free/open tooling with well-documented file formats so that AI agents can read, edit, and generate project assets reliably.

The repository already includes a Godot AI integration plugin (`addons/godot_ai`), reflecting our commitment to an AI-assisted development workflow. We believe AI collaboration accelerates progress without compromising quality or creative vision — and we're happy to have it in the pipeline.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from everyone, of every skill level and background.

### What We Look For

- **Code** — GDScript for gameplay, tools, and systems.
- **Art** — pixel art sprites, tiles, animations, concept art.
- **Audio** — music, sound effects, ambience.
- **Design** — creature designs, regional maps, battle balance.
- **Docs** — guides, tutorials, community resources.
- **Testing & QA** — finding bugs, balancing gameplay, giving feedback.
- **AI-assisted work** — prompts, pipelines, and tooling that make development easier.

### Community Standards

- **Be welcoming and respectful.** Harassment, discrimination, and toxicity are never tolerated.
- **Be constructive.** Give actionable feedback; take feedback graciously.
- **Collaborate.** Credit contributors, respect others' work, and help each other learn.
- **Open by default.** Where possible, prefer open licenses and shared assets.
- **Follow our contribution guide** (to be published in `CONTRIBUTING.md`) and our **Code of Conduct** (to be published in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`).
